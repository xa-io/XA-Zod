# XA Zod

XA Zod is a private early beta testing environment for XA Slave functionality.

Access may be available through contributions to Aethertek collaboration, support through Ko-fi, or donations. Contact [Aethertek](https://aethertek.io) to discuss participation and access arrangements.

Not every feature will become public. Work tested in Zod may remain private for many months, and some functionality may remain exclusive to Zod. Support and collaboration do not promise a public release schedule.

The public download provides an introduction to XA Zod with participation information and buttons to [join Discord](https://discord.gg/g2NmYxPQCa) or [visit Aethertek](https://aethertek.io). Downloading it does not grant private beta access. Open the window with `/xazod`, `/zod`, or the plugin configuration button.
